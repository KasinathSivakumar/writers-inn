OUR STORY

Heritage, Hospitality & Home.

Writer's Inn is a heritage homestay nestled in the peaceful town of Vaikom, Kerala. Designed to blend timeless architecture with modern comfort, every stay reflects warmth, authenticity and local charm.

Whether you're exploring Kerala's backwaters or simply seeking a quiet retreat, Writer's Inn offers an experience that feels personal, elegant and unforgettable.
